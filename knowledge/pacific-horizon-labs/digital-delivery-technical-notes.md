# Digital Delivery Technical Notes

Development seed. Source: Photo Booth analytics semantics used by Pacific Rising Ops.

## Delivery sessions

Each booth capture creates a delivery session. A session count is the number of delivery sessions created; it is not a count of unique guests.

## Session states

A ready session reached ready state. A session still in preparing state is unfinalized; the current telemetry does not record why, so unfinalized sessions are not described as failed uploads.

## Claim page and downloads

Guests open a claim page to view their media. A download click means the download control was clicked; it does not prove the file was saved to the guest's device.

## Social and review links

Instagram and TikTok numbers are outbound call-to-action clicks, not follows. Google Review CTA clicks are clicks on the review link, not submitted reviews.
