# Pacific Horizon Labs Company Overview

Development seed. Source: business.md.

## What we do

Pacific Horizon Labs rents open-air modern photo booths with studio-quality lighting and instant sharing. Services include digital capture (GIFs, Boomerangs and high-resolution still photos), physical prints on site (2x6 strips or 4x6 postcards), custom event branding, overlays and personalized tap-to-start screens, and premium physical and digital backdrop options.

## Service area

Primary coverage is Southern California: Orange County, Los Angeles County, and parts of Riverside and San Diego Counties. Travel within the standard radius is included; a nominal travel fee applies beyond it. The exact radius is not yet set.

## Operating hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time. Event operations run on weekends.

## Pacific Rising Ops

Pacific Rising Ops is the company's AI operations system. It drafts proposed email replies, but a draft is never authorization to send: sending requires explicit owner approval.
