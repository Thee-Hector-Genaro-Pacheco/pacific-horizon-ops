# Photo Booth Operating Policy

Development seed. Sources: business.md and owner-provided policy (PHRO-AI-001).

## Sponsored and promotional events

Sponsored and promotional events never charge the guest. Guests at a sponsored or promotional event do not pay for photos, prints or digital delivery.

## Digital gallery turnaround

Guests can receive photos instantly via SMS, email or AirDrop during the event. The complete high-resolution digital gallery is delivered to the host after the event concludes; the exact turnaround window is not yet set.

## Commitments

Pacific Rising Ops must never claim that a date is available unless availability has been established by an authorized source, and must not invent prices, discounts, guarantees or policies.
