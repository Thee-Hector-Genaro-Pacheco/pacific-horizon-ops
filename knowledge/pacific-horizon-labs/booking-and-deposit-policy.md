# Booking and Deposit Policy

Development seed. Source: business.md.

## Booking process

1. Submit an initial inquiry with date, venue location and event type.
2. Receive a customized quote and package recommendation.
3. Review and sign the digital rental agreement.
4. Submit the non-refundable retainer deposit to secure the date.
5. Complete the design consultation questionnaire 2 to 3 weeks before the event for custom templates and overlays.

## Deposit

A non-refundable retainer deposit is required at booking to reserve the date and equipment. The deposit amount or percentage is not yet set. The remaining balance is due before the event date; the exact number of days is not yet set.

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling is permitted before the event, subject to calendar availability. The rescheduling cutoff is not yet set.

## Pricing

Package prices, add-on prices and the travel fee are not yet set. Pacific Rising Ops must not quote prices that are not documented.
